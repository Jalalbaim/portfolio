Hello! I'm Mohamed Jalal Baim, I work at the intersection of machine learning, trustworthy AI, and causal inference, focusing on building robust and interpretable models that remain reliable under adversarial conditions. Currently pursuingmy MSc at École Polytechnique (l'X) in Applied Mathematics and Statistics, and working as a Researcher at BNP Paribas as a part of my end-of-studies internship.

My research interests are broadly centered around trustworthy AI, machine learning robustness, causal inference, and adversarial learning. I am particularly interested in how causal and probabilistic perspectives can help design more reliable and interpretable systems, especially in the presence of adversarial threats such as data poisoning and attacks on large language models. My current work focuses on developing causality-based defenses to detect and mitigate such vulnerabilities, with the goal of building AI systems that remain robust in complex, real-world environments.
